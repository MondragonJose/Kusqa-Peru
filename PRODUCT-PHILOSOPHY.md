# Product Philosophy — KUSQA

KUSQA is a civic-territorial platform for Peruvian youth to organize, act, and build community power at the district level.

## Tone Guardrails

Every piece of copy, UI text, and notification MUST adhere to these principles:

| Principle              | Do                                                                  | Don't                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cálido (Warm)**      | Speak like a peer; use "tú", "vamos", "tu distrito"                 | Institutional register ("se informa a los ciudadanos"), cold imperative          |
| **Territorial**        | Name specific districts, regions, places; reference local reality   | Generic civic language, abstract "community" without place                       |
| **Youth-native**       | Short sentences, emoji where appropriate, aspirational but grounded | Bureaucratic jargon, legalese, passive voice                                     |
| **Anti-institucional** | Empowerment language: "tú puedes", "tu voz", "tu huella"            | Government-like framing ("trámite", "solicitud", "registro"), top-down authority |
| **Action-oriented**    | Verbs over nouns: "limpia", "plantea", "únete"                      | Nominalization ("realización de actividades de limpieza")                        |
| **Low barrier**        | Assume no prior civic experience; explain simply                    | Assume political or technical literacy                                           |

## What KUSQA is NOT

- A government petition platform
- A protest organizer
- A formal NGO registry
- A bureaucratic workflow tool

## What KUSQA IS

- A warm invitation to territorial action
- A way to find your people in your district
- A narrative engine that turns participation into identity ("exploradora territorial", "tejedora de territorio")
- A trust layer built on real, verified local action

## Register Reference

| Context      | Tone                         | Example                                                                |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Empty state  | Warm invitation              | "Aún no hay rutas activas en Barranco. ¿Te animas a crear la primera?" |
| Error        | Empathetic, not blaming      | "Uy, algo no salió bien. Intenta de nuevo."                            |
| Success      | Celebratory, communal        | "¡Bacán! Tu propuesta ya está en el feed de Surquillo."                |
| Onboarding   | Concierge-like, step-by-step | "Vamos paso a paso. Primero, ¿qué causa te mueve?"                     |
| Notification | Direct, useful, from a peer  | "María se unió a tu misión en Magdalena."                              |

## Enforcement

- All new copy must pass the "¿le hablarías así a tu primo?" test.
- UI reviews check for register compliance.
- Generated/LLM copy must be reviewed against this document before merging.
- Violations in PRs are blocking (request changes, do not approve).
